November 2026 · Make of the Month

Fall Stitching

A little thread, a little creativity, and a whole lot of fall color.

Guest maker · Katherine Diuguid

Give it a stitch!

This November, we're welcoming textile artist Katherine Diuguid to the Tank for a fall-inspired embroidery project.

Learn a few stitches, play with autumn colors, and make an embroidered acorn of your own.

No experience needed. Just come ready to try something new!

The project

What we'll make

A fall acorn stitched with colorful thread onto a wooden shape. It's a fun introduction to embroidery and a great little seasonal keepsake.

In the Tank

Learn from a maker

Katherine Diuguid is a talented textile artist and teacher who'll guide us through the process in Open Lab.
