Two-Color Maker Badge

Level up from single-color printing: design a personalized badge, assign two PLA colors in Bambu Studio, and print it on the Bambu Lab A1 with AMS Lite.

The mission

One badge. Two colors.

Create a badge approximately 70 × 40 mm with a contrasting base and raised lettering. Learn to map two filaments, inspect color transitions, and complete a successful multicolor print. A simple flat badge keeps filament waste and print time manageable.

Two-Color Maker Badge

Machine: Bambu Lab A1 + AMS Lite · Material: PLA in two approved colors

Name: __________________________________ Grade: ______

  1. Plan your badge. Choose a name or short word and a simple two-color design. Use one color for the base and another for the raised lettering.
  2. Design the base. In Tinkercad, create a 70 × 40 × 3 mm badge with rounded corners if desired.
  3. Add raised details. Add text or a simple symbol on top, raised about 1 mm. Keep every feature connected to the base.
  4. Export the model. Export your completed model as STL and name it clearly.
  5. Open Bambu Studio. Import the model, select the correct Bambu Lab A1 printer, build plate, nozzle, and PLA profile.
  6. Assign two colors. With your instructor, confirm two PLA filaments are loaded in AMS Lite. Use the color painting tool or separate parts to assign base and lettering colors.
  7. Slice and inspect. Slice the model. Check the layer/color preview, supports, estimated time, purge waste, and that the second color appears where intended.
  8. Print and certify. Get instructor approval before printing. Monitor the job, let the plate cool, remove the badge safely, and show the completed two-color result.

Final checkpoint: □ Both colors correct   □ Badge printed successfully   □ Instructor accepted

Instructor initials: __________   Date: __________

Follow the build sequence

Check each stage as you finish it. Your instructor must approve the sliced preview and finished badge.

Maker checkpoint: Use only instructor-approved PLA and AMS Lite setup. Keep hands away from moving parts and the hot nozzle or build plate. Instructor approval is required before starting the print.
