The Maker Sign

Design and make a personalized 7 × 3 inch sign using the xTool P3. Learn three operations: engrave, score, and cut.

The mission

Design it. Engrave it. Cut it.

Create a small sign with your name, a short phrase, and one simple graphic. Engrave the lettering, score a decorative border or graphic, and cut out the sign. Start with a 7 × 3 inch rectangle; make the design your own.

The Maker Sign

Machine: xTool P3 · Material: Instructor-approved laser plywood · Size: 7 × 3 inches

Name: _________________________________ Grade: ______

  1. Plan your sign. Choose your name, a short phrase, and a simple vector graphic. Sketch the layout before starting.
  2. Open xTool Creative Space. Create a new project and confirm the xTool P3 is selected.
  3. Create the outline. Draw a 7 × 3 inch rectangle (177.8 × 76.2 mm). This will be your outside cut line.
  4. Add engraved details. Add your name and phrase. Keep text readable and away from the cut edge. Set these elements to Engrave.
  5. Add a scored detail. Draw a border or simple line-art graphic. Set it to Score, not Cut.
  6. Assign the cut. Set only the outer rectangle to Cut. Check that every element has the correct operation.
  7. Prepare the P3. With the instructor, identify the approved material and thickness, check placement and focus, confirm exhaust and ventilation, and use approved settings for the material. Never guess settings.
  8. Preview and get approval. Check the processing preview, placement and boundaries. Have the instructor verify the design, settings and safety setup before starting.
  9. Run and monitor. Follow the instructor’s operating procedure. Stay with the machine while it runs; stop the job and alert the instructor immediately for flame, excessive smoke or unexpected behavior.
  10. Inspect and certify. Wait until the machine is safe to open as directed. Check that the engraving is legible, score is clean, and outline cut is complete. Show the finished sign to the instructor.

Final checkpoint: □ Engrave correct   □ Score correct   □ Cut correct   □ Instructor approved
