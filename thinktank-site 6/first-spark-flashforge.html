Personalized Keychain

Machine: FlashForge Adventurer 5M Pro · Single-Color 3D Printing

Mission: Design and successfully print a personalized keychain. Your finished keychain is your certification project.

Name: __________________________________ Grade: ______

Video tutorial: Watch the keychain walkthrough ↗

  1. Open Tinkercad. Go to tinkercad.com. Sign in using your class account (class join link) and select Create New Design.
  2. Create your keychain base. Drag a Box or Cylinder onto the workplane. Resize it to a practical keychain shape, approximately 40 mm wide. Make the base thick enough to print securely.
  3. Personalize it. Drag in a Text shape, type your name or initials, and position the text on the base. Check that the lettering is attached to the base.
  4. Add a key-ring hole. Place a Cylinder set to Hole at one end. Set its diameter to about 5 mm and leave enough material around the hole. Select the parts and Group; confirm the hole cuts through the base.
  5. Export the design. Inspect your model, then use Export to save an STL file (or OBJ if instructed).
  6. Prepare your print. Open your model in FlashPrint on the printer computer. Check the size and placement, select the correct printer and material, and Slice. Review the preview before printing.
  7. Print safely. Have the ThinkTank instructor approve the setup before starting the print. Connect to the printer as directed, start the print, and monitor its progress. Do not touch hot parts.
  8. Finish your certification. Allow the print to cool. Remove and inspect your keychain. Show the completed keychain to the ThinkTank instructor; the finished, accepted keychain is your single-color 3D printing sign-off.
